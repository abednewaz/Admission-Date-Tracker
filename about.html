About Admission Tracker

Admission Tracker is made for students preparing for undergraduate admission in Bangladesh. Here is how to use the website effectively.

Find what you need

Use the search bar to search by university, unit, or date. You can search things like “DU”, “BUET”, or “16 Jan” to quickly find relevant admission information.

Check your requirements

Open a university card and tap Check Requirements. Select your academic group to see the available eligibility information. If something has not been officially verified, the site will clearly show that instead of guessing.

Mark your target universities

Use Mark on the universities you care about. Your marked list helps you keep track of your targets and powers the personalized calendar and reminders.

Use the Admission Calendar

The calendar highlights exam dates for your marked universities. Tap a marked date to see the university, unit, exam date, and available time information.

Check the Live Notice Board

The Notice Board keeps recent admission-related notices in one place. On mobile, tap the Notice Board header to expand the latest notices. Open the notice and verify important details with the official university source.

Turn on notifications

Allow browser notifications if you want admission reminders. For marked universities, reminders can appear one week, two days, one day, and one hour before an upcoming exam.

Tip: Notification permission must be allowed in your browser/device settings, and the website should remain available in your browser for these reminders.

Install Admission Tracker

On a supported mobile browser, use the Install / Add to Home screen option when available. Once installed, you can open Admission Tracker from your phone like an app for quicker access.

Keep an eye on changes

Admission dates, deadlines, exam times, locations, and requirements can change. Check the Notice Board and your marked universities regularly, especially as an exam gets closer.

Simple routine: Mark your universities → check the calendar → enable notifications → check the Notice Board → verify important changes from the official source.
