About Admission Tracker

Admission Tracker is built for students preparing for undergraduate admission in Bangladesh. This page shows the easiest way to get the most out of it.

Search universities and dates

Use the search bar to search by university, unit, or date. Try “DU”, “BUET”, “GST”, or a date such as “16 Jan” to quickly find what you need.

Check admission requirements

Open a university card and tap Check Requirements. Select your academic group to see the available eligibility information. If something has not been officially verified, it will be clearly marked rather than guessed.

Mark your target universities

Tap Mark University on the universities you are considering. Your marked universities are used for your personal admission calendar and exam reminders.

Use the Admission Calendar

The calendar highlights exam dates for your marked universities. Tap a marked date to see the university, unit, exam date, and available time information. It gives you a quick view of busy admission days.

Check the Live Notice Board

The Notice Board collects recent admission-related notices in one place. On mobile, tap the Notice Board bar to open the latest notices. Use it to spot new announcements, then open the notice and verify important details from the official source.

Turn on notifications

Allow browser notifications if you want reminders for your marked universities. Reminders can appear one week, two days, one day, and one hour before an upcoming exam.

Tip: When your browser asks for notification permission, choose Allow. You can manage notification permission later from your browser or device settings.

Install it on your phone

On a supported mobile browser, use the Install Admission Tracker option when it appears. If it does not appear automatically, open your browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. After installation, you can open it from your phone like an app.

Keep checking as admission season moves forward

Admission dates, deadlines, exam times, locations, and requirements can change. Check your marked universities, calendar, and Notice Board regularly—especially during the days leading up to an exam.

Simple routine: Search your universities → Mark your targets → Check the calendar → Enable notifications → Check the Notice Board → Verify important changes from the official source.
