Disclaimer

Admission Tracker is an independent information service for students. It is not a university or admission authority and is not affiliated with any university unless explicitly stated.

Information and accuracy

We aim to publish admission dates, deadlines, exam schedules, requirements, and notices using official university or admission-authority sources. Dates and requirements can change, and automated checking can have technical limitations.

We do not intentionally guess unpublished admission information. Where a detail cannot be safely verified, it may be shown as Not published yet or Better check Yourself.

Always verify important details

Before paying an application fee, travelling to an exam centre, submitting an application, or making another important decision, check the latest official notice or admission guideline from the relevant university or admission authority.

In simple terms: Admission Tracker helps you find and follow admission information, but the official university or admission authority is always the final authority.
