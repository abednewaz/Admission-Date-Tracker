How to use Admission Tracker

Quick guide to the main features.

1. Install the app (Optional)Want to use Admission Tracker like an app on your phone? Tap the Download / Install button in the Install box. This is optional — you can use the website normally without installing it.Install box → Download / Install
2. Turn on notificationsTap Enable Notifications to allow admission notices and exam alerts to reach you on your device.Notifications → Enable Notifications
3. SearchUse the search box to quickly find a university, unit, or admission exam date.Search → type university/unit/date
4. Mark a universityTap Mark University on an admission card. The university will be saved to your list and you can track its countdown and exam dates.Admission card → Mark University
5. Check requirementsTap Check Requirements to see the available admission eligibility information for your group.Admission card → Check Requirements
6. Official admission websiteUse the official-source button on a card to open the university's official admission page.Admission card → Official Admission Website
7. Notice BoardCheck the Notice Board for important recent admission notices in one place. Verify important information on the official source.Notice Board
8. My Admission CalendarMarked universities' exam dates appear on the calendar, making your upcoming admission schedule easier to track.My Admission Calendar
