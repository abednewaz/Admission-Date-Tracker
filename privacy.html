Privacy Policy

Admission Tracker is designed to provide university admission information without requiring you to create an account.

Information we collect

The site does not ask you to submit your name, phone number, password, academic records, or other personal profile information to use the main features.

Local browser storage

Some preferences and feature data may be stored locally in your browser, such as marked universities, notification status, theme preference, and related settings. This helps the site remember your choices.

Notifications

If you allow browser notifications, your browser and device control whether notifications can be delivered. You can revoke permission through your browser or device settings.

External services

The website may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, content delivery, or advertising when enabled. External university links are governed by the policies of those destinations.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's features or services change.
