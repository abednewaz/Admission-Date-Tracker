Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Information we collect

Admission Tracker is designed to work without requiring an account. The site does not ask students to submit names, phone numbers, passwords, or academic records to use the tracker.

Local browser storage

Some features use browser storage on your device, for example to remember marked universities, notification status, theme preference, and other local settings. This information is stored in your browser and is not submitted to Admission Tracker as an account profile.

Notifications

If you enable browser notifications, your browser controls the permission. Admission Tracker uses the permission to show admission reminders for universities you have marked.

Third-party services

The site may link to official university websites and may use third-party infrastructure such as hosting, analytics, advertising, or content-delivery services when enabled. Those services may process information according to their own policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated when site functionality changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.
