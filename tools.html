User Manual

Complete visual guide to the main Admission Tracker features.

8 STEPS
1. Install the appUse the Install box to add Admission Tracker to your phone like an app.Install box → Download / Install
2. Turn on notificationsEnable admission notices and exam alerts on your device.Notifications → Enable Notifications
3. SearchFind a university, unit, or admission exam date quickly.Search → type university/unit/date
4. Mark a universityMark a target university to track its countdown and exam dates.Admission card → Mark University
5. Check requirementsSee available admission eligibility information for your group.Admission card → Check Requirements
6. Official admission websiteOpen the university's official admission page from its card.Admission card → Official Admission Website
7. Notice BoardCheck recent admission notices and verify important details from official sources.Notice Board
8. My Admission CalendarSee exam dates for marked universities in one calendar.My Admission Calendar
Prototype mode: tool actions, persistence, account state, and automated reminders are intentionally disabled. Visual components remain complete.
